## Sign in and Overview

Welcome to PremaDesk. This walkthrough shows how hotel managers and general managers can oversee daily operations. Sign in with your work email and password. If you manage more than one hotel, select the right one from **Property**. On **Overview**, check today’s arrivals, departures, and room readiness so you know where the team needs help.

## Rooms and Maintenance

Open **Rooms** to check which rooms are in service, their housekeeping status, and their nightly rates. Select **Add room** to enter a room number, type, capacity, and rate. Use **Edit** to update details or take a room out of service so it can’t be booked.

Open **Maintenance** to report a problem or review open issues. Once the repair is complete, select **Mark fixed**. The issue stays in the history with its fixed date, so you can check when the work was done.

## Reservations and Folios

Open **Reservations** and search by guest name or filter by status. Here you can create bookings, check guests in and out, and review their balances under **Folio**. For example, if a guest was overcharged, select **View receipt** for the original payment, enter the cash refund amount and reason, and select **Record cash refund**. Open your own cash shift before recording the refund so it’s included in the till reconciliation.

## Guests

Open **Guests** to find or add a guest profile. Select **View details** to check stay history and past room numbers before helping a returning guest. Use **Edit** to update contact details or record a preference for their next visit.

## Cash desk

Open **Cash desk** to review cash shifts for the hotel. On a closed shift, compare **Counted** with **Expected**. The **Variance** shows the difference, so you can follow up if the till is short or over. To open your own shift, enter the cash already in the till as the **Opening float** and select **Open shift**. At closing, enter the actual cash count and select **Close shift**.

## Staff

Open **Staff** to manage front desk and housekeeping access. Enter the staff member’s name, email, role, and initial password, then select **Add staff member**. Use **Edit** to update their role, or **Suspend** to pause access. If someone forgets their password, select **Reset password** and share the single-use code privately. It expires after 30 minutes.

## Reports

Open **Reports** to review how rooms are performing. Set the start date and the first date to exclude, then select **Run report**. Use room nights booked and occupancy to see demand. Check room revenue, average daily rate, and revenue per available room to understand how bookings contribute to revenue.

## Change password and sign out

Open **Change password** to update your own password. Enter your current password, confirm a new one of at least 12 characters, and select **Update password**. Sign in again with the new password. When you’re done, select **Sign out**.
