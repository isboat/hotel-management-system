# PremaDesk promotional videos

Short videos and a shareable brochure for hotel and guesthouse owners in Ghana. Each video includes narration and on-screen captions.

- **A Simpler Day at the Front Desk** (49 seconds). Shows bookings, room readiness, cash payments, and receipts. [Video (MP4)](media/PremaDesk-promo-front-desk.mp4?raw=true) · [Captions (SRT)](media/PremaDesk-promo-front-desk.srt?raw=true)
- **See What’s Happening at Your Hotel** (47 seconds). Shows cash reconciliation, repair history, and occupancy and revenue reporting. [Video (MP4)](media/PremaDesk-promo-for-owners.mp4?raw=true) · [Captions (SRT)](media/PremaDesk-promo-for-owners.srt?raw=true)
- **A Warm Welcome** (39 seconds). Shows how staff can confirm bookings, check room readiness, and look up returning guests’ stay history. [Video (MP4)](media/PremaDesk-promo-a-warm-welcome.mp4?raw=true) · [Captions (SRT)](media/PremaDesk-promo-a-warm-welcome.srt?raw=true)
- **Close the Day with Confidence** (44 seconds). Shows how owners can track cash in Ghana cedis, reconcile the till, and use occupancy and room revenue to guide pricing. [Video (MP4)](media/PremaDesk-promo-close-with-confidence.mp4?raw=true) · [Captions (SRT)](media/PremaDesk-promo-close-with-confidence.srt?raw=true)

**Hotel Owner Brochure** (one A4 page). Covers bookings, housekeeping, cash, and reporting. [Download the brochure (PDF)](media/PremaDesk-hotel-owner-brochure.pdf?raw=true)
