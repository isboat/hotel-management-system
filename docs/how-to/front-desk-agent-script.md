## Sign in

Welcome to PremaDesk. This walkthrough shows how a receptionist can manage bookings, guest records, and cash payments. Enter your email and password, then select **Sign in**. If you work at more than one hotel, choose the right one from **Property**.

## Overview

Start on **Overview** to see what needs attention today. Check the arrivals, departures, and rooms ready for guests. Select **View reservations** to open the booking list, or **Room inventory** to check individual rooms.

## Rooms

On **Rooms**, use **Find a room** to search by number or room type. Check the rate, how many guests it sleeps, and its housekeeping status. This helps you confirm whether the room is ready before a guest arrives.

## Cash desk

Before recording a cash payment, open **Cash desk**. Enter the **Opening float** in Ghana cedis and select **Open shift**. The opening float is the cash already in the till, including money set aside for change.

## Reservations

To book a stay, select **New reservation**, enter the arrival and departure dates, then select **Find rooms**. Search for an existing guest profile, or enter a name to create a new profile with the booking. Choose an available room, select **Confirm reservation**, and check the details in the pop-up before confirming.

To find a booking later, search by guest name or filter by status. When the guest arrives and the room is ready, select **Check in**. If a booking is cancelled, select **Cancel booking** and confirm. A booking with a cash payment still on its folio must be settled before it can be cancelled.

### Folio and checkout

Select **Folio** on the booking to review the guest’s charges and balance. To add an extra, such as laundry, enter the description and amount, then select **Post charge** and confirm.

To record a cash payment, enter how much to apply to the balance and how much cash the guest handed you. Select **Record cash payment**. Check the change shown in the pop-up and confirm. You can then print the receipt. Once the balance is paid, select **Check out** on the booking when the guest leaves.

## Guests

Open **Guests** to find a returning guest, or select **Add guest** to create a profile. Use **View details** to see their contact information and stay history, including room numbers. Choose **Edit** to update a phone number or note a preference for a future stay.

## Cash desk at the end of a shift

Return to **Cash desk**, count the cash in the till, and enter the total under **Cash counted**. Select **Close shift**. Compare the counted amount with the expected amount to see whether there’s a difference.

## Change password and sign out

To change your password, open **Change password**. Enter your current password and confirm a new one with at least 12 characters. Select **Update password**, then sign in again with the new password. When you finish using PremaDesk, select **Sign out**.
