About us

Built around the way independent hotels work.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is PremaDesk for?

Independent hotels and guesthouses in Ghana that want to manage day-to-day operations in one place.

Can we record cash payments in Ghana cedis?

Yes. Your team can record cash payments and refunds in Ghana cedis, print receipts, and reconcile an opening float against the actual cash count at shift close.

Do staff need to install an app?

PremaDesk is a web application. Staff sign in through a browser on a phone, tablet, or laptop.

Can I see how the hotel is performing?

Managers can run a date-range report with room nights booked, occupancy, room revenue, average daily rate, and revenue per available room.
